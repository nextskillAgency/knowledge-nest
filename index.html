Universities

Entry Tests, Schedules & Eligibility

Verified basics for 9 major Pakistani universities. Schedules repeat every year with small changes — always confirm final dates on the official site.

N

NUST

Islamabad
  • TestNET (NUST Entry Test) — computer-based; SAT/ACT also accepted
  • When4 series yearly, roughly Oct – Jul; best score counts
  • EligibilityMin 60% in SSC & HSSC
  • MeritNET 75% · HSSC 15% · SSC 10%
Official Admissions Site
F

FAST (NUCES)

Islamabad · Lahore · Karachi · Peshawar
  • TestFAST Admission Test (NU Test); SAT accepted (1000–1200 min)
  • WhenFall: tests late Jun – early Jul; Spring cycle: tests in Dec
  • EligibilityMin 60% in SSC & HSSC
  • NoteResult-awaiting students can apply
Official Admissions Site
G

GIKI

Topi, Swabi
  • TestGIKI Admission Test (~80 MCQs: Maths, Physics, English)
  • WhenApplications Mar – mid Jun; test early Jul
  • EligibilityMin 60% FSc Pre-Engg / ICS
  • MeritTest ~85% · Academics ~15%
Official Admissions Site
C

COMSATS University

Islamabad · Lahore · Wah · Abbottabad & more
  • TestNTS NAT (valid 1 year) or CUI's own Special NAT
  • WhenFall: NAT Jul – Aug · Spring: NAT Dec – Jan
  • EligibilityProgram-wise — check official criteria
  • NoteTwo intakes every year
Official Admissions Site
A

Air University

Islamabad
  • TestAU-CBT (computer-based); NAT / USAT / SAT-I (1100+) exempt
  • WhenFall: CBT mid – late Jul; unlimited retakes, best score counts
  • EligibilityProgram-wise — check official criteria
  • CentersIslamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Multan, Kamra, Kharian
Official Admissions Site
P

PIEAS

Islamabad
  • TestPIEAS Admission Test (100 MCQs, 3 hrs; +3 / −1 marking)
  • WhenTwo tests: April & July
  • EligibilityMin 60% in Matric & FSc (Pre-Engg / ICS)
  • MeritTest 60% · FSc-I 25% · SSC 15%
Official Admissions Site
I

ITU

Lahore
  • TestITU Admission Test; SAT-I / SAT-II accepted
  • WhenApplications close ~mid Jun; test early Jul
  • EligibilityMin 60% in Matric & Intermediate (most BS programs)
  • SelectionAcademics 40% · Test 40% · Interview 20%
Official Website
U

Punjab University

Lahore
  • TestPU Admission Test — mandatory for all UG programs (7 streams)
  • WhenTwo tests: mid Jun & mid Jul; 100 MCQs, no negative marking
  • EligibilityHSSC passed or result-awaiting
  • WeightageTest counts 25% in merit
Official Website
E

UET Lahore

Lahore (Punjab-wide ECAT)
  • TestECAT — 100 MCQs in 100 min, computer-based, no negative marking
  • WhenPhase-I: Mar – Apr · Phase-II: mid – late Jun
  • EligibilityMin 60% FSc (Engg) · 50% (Computing)
  • MeritMatric 17% · FSc 50% · ECAT 33%
Official Admissions Site
Note: Dates shown are typical annual patterns (2026 cycle reference). Universities announce exact dates each year — always confirm on the official admissions site before applying.
